02 — How it works

One sample,
six stages, one record.

Every stage is logged in our LIMS and shown on a single tracking screen, including who is responsible for it.

  1. 0.0 · SamploIntake

    An order number and barcoded kit are issued. Scanning the kit opens the tracking screen.

  2. 0.2 · SamploPrep & QC

    Arrival photo, temperature and per-sample QC. If a sample falls short, you decide before we proceed.

  3. 0.4 · PartnerSequencing

    Run by a vetted sequencing partner, shown on the same screen from start to finish.

  4. 0.6 · SamploData delivery

    Raw data and QC report on the portal. If the ETA moves, we tell you why, first.

  5. 0.8 · OmicsMateAnalysis

    AI completes the first pass; vetted experts bid on interpretation.

  6. 1.0 · Samplo · DxBiobank & clinic

    Leftover samples are banked and can feed biomarker programs, within consent.

06 — FAQ

Questions we hear

01Can I send samples today?
Not yet. Services launch in Korea in stages from 2026. Tell us your sample type, count and planned assay, and we'll let you know when and how we can take them.
02Do you accept samples from outside Korea?
Our first phase serves labs and hospitals in Korea. International shipments depend on sample type, import permits and cold-chain logistics, so we review them case by case. Get in touch before you ship anything.
03Do you sequence in-house?
Prep, QC, records, tracking and storage are done by us. Sequencing goes to vetted partners and in-depth analysis to OmicsMate experts. The tracking screen always shows who is handling each stage.
04Who owns the samples and data?
You do. Biobanked samples are used only at your request and within consent. Scope of use is written into the contract, and access is closed when the project ends.
05How do pharma collaborations start?
With a short, non-confidential intro: stage of interest, indication and modality at a level you can share, and the samples or data you need. We sign an NDA before any detailed discussion.
06When will pricing be published?
At launch. Samplo will offer per-order, lab membership and institutional plans. OmicsMate already shows cost bands in its estimator.
